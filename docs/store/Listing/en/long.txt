<b>Video Converter & Compressor</b> uses FFmpeg to convert, compress, and trim videos right on your phone. No watermarks, no video uploads, open source.

Convert MOV, MKV, AVI, or WebM to MP4, turn MP4 into MP3, shrink large videos to save storage, resize them for sharing, or extract the audio as MP3.

<b>Your videos stay on your device</b>
All conversion happens locally on your phone. Your videos are not uploaded to any server or cloud service.

<b>Two modes for every job</b>

⚡ <b>Normal Mode</b>
Pick a quality preset and convert to MP4. Ideal for quickly reducing file size and freeing up storage.

⚙️ <b>Expert Mode</b>
Use advanced <b>FFmpeg settings</b>: Choose the codec and output format, set the quality with CRF or a target bitrate, and adjust resolution, aspect ratio, frame rate, and audio settings.

<b>Supported output formats</b>
• Video: MP4, MKV, MOV, WebM, TS, AVI
• Audio: MP3, AAC, AC3, Opus, Vorbis

<b>Features</b>

🚫 <b>No watermarks</b>
Your videos stay clean, without logos or overlays.

📉 <b>Compress videos</b>
Reduce video size and save storage space on your device.

🎬 <b>Convert formats</b>
Convert videos to common formats, including MP4, MKV, and WebM.

🎵 <b>Extract audio</b>
Save the sound of a video as an audio file, for example as MP3.

✂️ <b>Trim videos</b>
Set exact start and end points before converting.

💬 <b>Add subtitles</b>
Add SRT or ASS subtitles in Expert Mode, burned into the picture or as a switchable track.

🎛️ <b>Modern codecs</b>
Encode with H.264, H.265, VP9, or AV1. On supported devices, H.265 can use the hardware encoder for much faster conversions.

🎨 <b>HDR support</b>
HDR videos can stay in HDR with H.265 or AV1, or be converted to SDR with H.264.

📊 <b>Preview and size estimate</b>
Preview your video and see the estimated file size before converting.

💎 <b>FFmpeg on Android</b>
Use proven <b>FFmpeg</b> technology directly in a simple Android app.

✨ <b>Remove ads</b>
A one-time in-app purchase removes ads permanently and supports the project.
